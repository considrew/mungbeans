Three things are true of every link on this page. I pay for the tool with my own
money. The referral pays mungbeans.io a commission if you subscribe, at no extra
cost to you. And nothing here was written or altered in exchange for
compensation.

That last one is the part worth defending. A referral gives me an interest in
your subscribing, which is exactly the interest that produces bad reviews of
good products and worse reviews of bad ones. The defense is not a promise about
my character. It is that this page names what each tool cannot do alongside what
it can, and that the same disclosure sits on every placement elsewhere on the
site. If a tool stops earning its keep it comes off the page, referral or not.
